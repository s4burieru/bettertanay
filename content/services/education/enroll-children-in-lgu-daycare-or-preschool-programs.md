# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs — Tanay, Rizal

The Municipality of Tanay, Rizal, through the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** and barangays, operates **daycare centers** and supports **public preschool education** for young children.

---

## 1) LGU Daycare Programs

### What’s Offered

- Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) programs for children aged **3–4 years old**.
- Play-based learning activities focused on socialization, basic literacy, creativity, and motor skills.
- Health monitoring and nutrition-related activities for enrolled children.
- Supplementary feeding programs in selected barangays.

### Who Can Join

- Children aged **3–4 years old** residing in the barangay.
- Priority may be given to low-income or identified beneficiary families.

### Requirements (Typical)

- Child’s **Birth Certificate** (PSA or Local Civil Registrar copy).
- **Barangay Certificate** or proof of residency.
- Recent ID photo of the child.
- Accomplished **Daycare Enrollment Form** from the daycare center.

### How to Enroll

1. Visit your **Barangay Daycare Center** or **Barangay Hall** to inquire about enrollment schedules.
2. Submit the required documents and complete the registration form.
3. Attend parent or guardian orientation before the start of classes.

**Cost:** Free (LGU-funded), though some barangays may request voluntary contributions for school supplies or activities.

---

## 2) Public Preschool / Kindergarten Programs

### What’s Offered

- The Department of Education (DepEd) **Kindergarten Program** for children aged **5 years old**.
- School readiness programs focusing on literacy, numeracy, communication, and social development.
- Learning activities conducted in public elementary schools.

### Who Can Join

- Children who are **5 years old** by the opening of the school year.
- Residents within the school’s service or catchment area.

### Requirements (Typical DepEd Requirements)

- Child’s **PSA Birth Certificate**.
- Proof of residency or **Barangay Certificate**.
- Recent 2×2 ID photo.
- Accomplished **Kindergarten Registration Form** from the school.

### How to Enroll

1. Proceed to the nearest **public elementary school** during the announced enrollment or early registration period.
2. Submit the required documents to the school registrar or teacher-in-charge.
3. Attend orientation sessions for parents and guardians.

**Cost:** Free (government-funded).

---

## Contact & Updates

- **Barangay Daycare Centers** — Announce daycare enrollment schedules and local requirements.
- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** — Oversees daycare and child development programs.
- **DepEd Tanay District / Public Elementary Schools** — Handles kindergarten registration and enrollment.

---

## Quick Tips

- Enroll early since daycare and kindergarten slots may be limited.
- Prepare photocopies of your child’s birth certificate and residency documents ahead of time.
- Attend orientation sessions to learn school policies and child development programs.
- Coordinate with barangay officials for updated enrollment schedules and requirements.
