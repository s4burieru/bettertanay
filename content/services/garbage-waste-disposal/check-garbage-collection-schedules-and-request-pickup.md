# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Tanay, Rizal manages solid waste collection through the **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** in coordination with barangays, following the guidelines of RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act).

Residents are encouraged to follow proper waste segregation and observe their barangay’s collection schedule.

---

## 1) Regular Garbage Collection

### How it works

- Garbage collection schedules vary **per barangay** (commonly 2–3 times per week depending on route and population).
- Municipal or accredited trucks collect:
  - Biodegradable waste
  - Recyclable waste
  - Residual/non-recyclable waste
- Proper segregation at source is required under national law.

### Where to check schedules

- **Barangay Hall** — official schedules are posted or announced per household/community
- **Barangay announcements / group chats** — updates on delays or changes
- **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** — for municipal-wide advisories

> Tip: Always confirm your barangay’s schedule, as routes may differ even between nearby sitios.

---

## 2) Special Garbage Pickup (Bulk or Non-Routine Waste)

Bulk waste is **not included in regular collection schedules**.

### How to request

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall**
2. Provide details:
   - Type of waste
   - Estimated volume
   - Exact pickup location
3. Barangay coordinates with **MENRO** or assigned haulers
4. Wait for scheduled pickup approval

### Items requiring special pickup

- Furniture (sofas, cabinets, mattresses)
- Appliances (refrigerators, TVs, washing machines)
- Construction debris and renovation waste
- Yard waste in large quantities
- Electronic waste (e-waste)

Some barangays may require coordination fees depending on volume and transport needs.

Contact via: Tanay Municipal Hall trunkline (ask for MENRO)

### Barangay Hall

Each barangay has an environment or sanitation officer responsible for:

- Scheduling waste collection
- Coordinating special pickups
- Enforcing segregation rules

---

## 4) Quick Tips for Residents

- Put out garbage **only on scheduled collection days**
- Always segregate:
  - Biodegradable
  - Recyclable
  - Residual
- Use sealed containers to avoid animals scattering trash
- Do not burn garbage (prohibited under RA 9003 and Clean Air Act)
- During heavy rains or disasters, expect schedule adjustments

---

## 5) Businesses and Establishments

- Must comply with segregation and municipal waste rules
- Large generators may need **private haulers**
- Maintain records of waste disposal if required during inspections

---

## Key Reminder

Proper waste segregation and following your barangay’s collection schedule helps prevent flooding, pests, and environmental pollution while keeping Tanay clean and sustainable.
