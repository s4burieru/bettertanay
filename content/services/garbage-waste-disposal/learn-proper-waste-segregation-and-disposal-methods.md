# Guidelines for Proper Waste Segregation and Disposal — Tanay, Rizal

Proper waste segregation is required in **Tanay, Rizal** under **Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000)** and municipal environmental ordinances. These rules help reduce flooding, prevent pollution, and maintain public health across all barangays.

---

## 1) Waste Categories You Must Separate

| Category                      | Examples                                                              | How to Dispose                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Biodegradable**             | Food scraps, vegetable/fruit peels, yard waste, paper towels          | Place in compostable/green bin; collected by barangay or municipal trucks; can also be composted at home or Barangay MRF |
| **Recyclable**                | Plastic bottles, glass, cans, paper, cartons                          | Clean and dry before storing; bring to junkshops or Barangay Materials Recovery Facility (MRF)                           |
| **Residual**                  | Diapers, sachets, styrofoam, contaminated plastics, broken ceramics   | Place in designated residual waste container; collected for disposal at sanitary landfill                                |
| **Special / Hazardous Waste** | Batteries, bulbs, e-waste, chemicals, paint containers, medical waste | Do not mix with regular waste; bring to special collection events or designated drop-off points                          |

---

## 2) General Rules for Segregation

- Always segregate waste at the source (household level)
- Use separate containers or labeled bags for each waste type
- Do not burn garbage (prohibited under RA 9003 and Clean Air Act)
- Place garbage out only on your barangay’s scheduled collection day
- Keep recyclables clean and dry to avoid pests and odor
- Construction debris must be handled by private haulers (not regular collection)

---

## 3) Barangay Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs)

- Every barangay in Tanay is encouraged to maintain or coordinate with an **MRF**
- MRFs handle:
  - Composting of biodegradable waste
  - Sorting and storage of recyclables
- Residents may bring segregated waste directly to their barangay MRF if allowed
- Some barangays may implement recycling incentive programs depending on local initiatives

---

## 4) For Businesses & Establishments

- Required to implement waste segregation within their premises
- Must comply with municipal environmental regulations
- Large generators (restaurants, resorts, factories) may need accredited private waste haulers
- Hazardous and special waste must be disposed of through authorized handlers
- Proof of proper disposal may be required during inspections

---

## 5) Penalties for Non-Compliance

- Improper disposal, littering, or open burning may result in fines under municipal ordinances and RA 9003
- Repeat violations may lead to penalties or business permit restrictions for establishments
- Illegal dumping in rivers, roads, or vacant areas is strictly prohibited

---

## 6) Quick Tips for Households

- Keep at least 3 bins: biodegradable, recyclable, residual
- Reduce plastic use by bringing reusable bags and containers
- Compost kitchen waste if possible
- Follow barangay collection schedules strictly
- Teach household members proper segregation habits
