# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Municipality of Tanay

Public market stalls in the Municipality of Tanay are managed by the **Municipal Government of Tanay** through the appropriate market administration and municipal business offices.

The main public market is the **Tanay Public Market** located in Barangay Plaza Aldea, near the town proper and transport terminal.

---

## How to Apply for a Public Market Stall

1. **Visit the Tanay Municipal Hall**
   - Inquire at the office handling public market operations or the Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO).

2. **Ask About Available Stalls**
   - Stall availability depends on vacancies and market demand.

3. **Prepare and Submit Requirements**
   Typical requirements may include:
   - Accomplished application form
   - Valid government-issued ID
   - Barangay Certificate of Residency
   - Recent 2x2 ID photos
   - Business Permit or proof of application/registration

4. **Wait for Evaluation and Approval**
   - Applications may undergo assessment, prioritization, or waiting-list procedures depending on stall availability.

5. **Pay the Required Fees**
   - Payments are usually made at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.

6. **Sign the Lease or Occupancy Agreement**
   - Approved applicants must sign the agreement before operating.

---

## Types of Stalls Commonly Available

Depending on availability, public market spaces may include:

- Vegetable and fruit stalls
- Meat section stalls
- Fish and seafood stalls
- Dry goods stalls
- Rice and grain stalls
- Carinderia or eatery spaces
- Commercial stalls and mini-shops

---

## Rental Fees and Charges

Rental fees vary depending on:

- Stall size
- Stall location within the market
- Market section
- Type of goods or services sold

Additional charges may include:

- Electrical fees
- Garbage and sanitation fees
- Regulatory and permit fees

For updated rates and schedules of payment, inquire directly at the Municipal Hall or market administration office.

---

## Rules and Responsibilities of Vendors

Market vendors are generally required to:

- Pay rental fees on time
- Maintain cleanliness and sanitation
- Follow market operating hours and municipal regulations
- Renew permits and clearances annually
- Comply with health and safety standards
- Avoid selling prohibited or regulated goods without authorization

Failure to comply with municipal market regulations may result in penalties or cancellation of stall occupancy.

## Negosyo Center Tanay Assistance

The **Negosyo Center Tanay** assists aspiring entrepreneurs and market vendors with:

- DTI Business Name Registration
- Business permit guidance
- MSME support programs
- Entrepreneurial seminars and training

## Important Offices

### Municipal Government of Tanay

Handles public market administration and municipal services.

### Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)

Processes business permits and related requirements.

### Municipal Treasurer’s Office

Handles rental fee payments and other municipal charges.

### Negosyo Center Tanay

Provides assistance for MSMEs and market vendors.
