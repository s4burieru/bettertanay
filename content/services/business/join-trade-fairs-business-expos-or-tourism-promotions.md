# Participate in Local Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotion Activities — Tanay, Rizal

Showcase your products, tourism services, and local brand by joining **municipal, provincial, and regional trade fairs, expos, festivals, and tourism promotion events** in and around Tanay, Rizal.

---

## Who Can Join

- MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises)
- Sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, and cooperatives
- Tourism-related businesses
- Local artisans and food businesses
- Farmers’ groups and livelihood associations
- Youth and women-led enterprises
- Startups and online sellers with local operations

> Priority is often given to registered businesses, local products, tourism enterprises, and community-based organizations.

---

## Common Event Types

### Trade Fairs & MSME Exhibits

- Municipal trade fairs
- Livelihood bazaars
- OTOP (One Town, One Product) showcases
- Food and product expos
- Startup and entrepreneurship events

### Tourism Promotion Activities

- Tourism festivals and cultural events
- Travel and eco-tourism showcases
- Farm tourism promotions
- Adventure and outdoor tourism campaigns
- Local heritage and arts festivals

### Community & Seasonal Bazaars

- Christmas bazaars
- Fiesta trade fairs
- Weekend markets
- School and mall exhibits

## How to Participate (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Prepare Business Documents

Typical requirements include:

- DTI / SEC / CDA Registration
- BIR Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)
- Mayor’s/Business Permit
- Barangay Clearance
- Product permits or certifications (if applicable)

For food products:

- FDA permits or sanitary compliance (if required)

For tourism businesses:

- DOT Accreditation (if applicable)

---

### 2. Watch for Event Announcements

Monitor announcements from:

- Municipality of Tanay
- Rizal Provincial Government
- DTI Rizal
- Tourism offices
- Negosyo Centers
- Local malls and event organizers

---

### 3. Submit an Application or Registration Form

You may be asked to provide:

- Business profile
- Product catalog or menu
- Booth requirements
- Photos or promotional materials
- Preferred event dates

---

### 4. Wait for Assessment & Confirmation

Organizers may evaluate:

- Product quality
- Branding and presentation
- Safety compliance
- Booth availability

Accepted participants may receive booth assignments and event guidelines.

---

### 5. Prepare for the Event

### Bring:

- Tarpaulins and signage
- Price lists
- Calling cards and brochures
- QR payment options (GCash, Maya, bank transfer)
- Product samples and inventory
- Extension cords and booth supplies

---

### 6. Participate During Event Days

- Arrive early during ingress/setup
- Follow organizer and safety rules
- Maintain cleanliness and customer service standards
- Capture customer inquiries and leads

---

### 7. Follow Up After the Event

After the event:

- Respond to customer inquiries
- Send quotations or catalogs
- Post event updates online
- Track sales and customer feedback

- Offer event-only promos or bundles.
