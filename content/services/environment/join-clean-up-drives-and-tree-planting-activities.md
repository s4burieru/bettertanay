# Join Clean-Up Drives and Tree Planting Activities in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Tanay, Rizal regularly conducts environmental programs such as community clean-up drives, river rehabilitation, and tree planting activities to protect natural resources, watersheds, and eco-tourism areas in the municipality.

These activities are usually coordinated by the **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** together with barangays and partner organizations.

---

## How to Participate

1. Watch for announcements from:
   - Barangay Hall
   - Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)
   - Official Tanay LGU pages and announcements

2. Register your name at:
   - Barangay Hall
   - MENRO (if city-wide or municipal-wide activity)

3. Attend on the scheduled date and location

4. Bring basic supplies such as gloves, water, and protective gear if needed

---

## Scheduled Activities (Typical Programs)

- **Barangay Clean-Up Drives** — regularly conducted per barangay (often monthly or quarterly)
- **River and Coastal Clean-Ups** — especially in areas near rivers and waterways in Tanay
- **Tree Planting Activities** — commonly held during:
  - Environment Month (June)
  - Special LGU and DENR-led programs
- **Watershed Protection Activities** — in coordination with environmental agencies

Schedules may vary depending on LGU programs and environmental conditions.

---

## Where to Register

**Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

You may also register through your **Barangay Hall** for local clean-up and tree planting activities.

---

## What to Bring

- Gloves (rubber or gardening gloves)
- Face mask (optional but recommended)
- Durable footwear or boots
- Drinking water
- Hat or sun protection
- Garbage bags (sometimes provided by LGU)

---

## Benefits

- Certificate of participation (for students or volunteers, if requested)
- Community service recognition (depending on program)
- Participation in environmental protection efforts
- In some tree planting events: free seedlings or planting materials

---

## Key Reminder

Joining clean-up drives and tree planting activities helps protect Tanay’s rivers, forests, and tourism areas, while strengthening community involvement in environmental conservation.
