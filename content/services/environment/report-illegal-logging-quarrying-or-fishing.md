# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Tanay, Rizal enforces environmental protection laws to safeguard its forests, rivers, watersheds, and natural resources. Residents are encouraged to report illegal activities that threaten the municipality’s environment and biodiversity.

---

## What You Can Report

- **Illegal logging** — unauthorized cutting or transport of trees in protected or public forest areas
- **Illegal quarrying** — unpermitted extraction of sand, gravel, or other natural materials
- **Illegal fishing** — use of dynamite, electricity, cyanide, fine-mesh nets, or fishing in restricted areas (rivers, lakes, protected zones)
- **Illegal dumping in natural areas** — waste disposal in rivers, forests, or protected land
- **Wildlife poaching** — hunting or capturing protected species

---

## How to Report

### Option 1 — Municipal Office

**Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

Reports may be submitted in person or coordinated through your Barangay Hall.

---

### Option 2 — Law Enforcement Agencies

- **PNP Tanay** — for illegal activities requiring immediate enforcement response
- **DENR CALABARZON (Region IV-A)** — for forest, mining, and protected area violations
- **Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)** — for illegal fishing activities in inland waters

---

### Option 3 — Barangay Level Reporting

You may also report directly to your Barangay Hall or barangay tanod. They can:

- Document the incident
- Coordinate with MENRO or PNP
- Assist in immediate response if needed

---

## Information to Provide

When reporting, include as much detail as safely possible:

- Exact location (barangay, landmarks, GPS if available)
- Date and time of incident
- Description of activity observed
- Number of individuals involved
- Vehicle details (plate number, type of vehicle), if applicable
- Photos or videos (only if safe to capture)

⚠️ Do not put yourself at risk when gathering evidence.

---

## Confidentiality

Reports may be submitted anonymously. Personal identity is kept confidential upon request to ensure reporter safety.

---

## Legal Basis

Violations are covered under national and environmental laws, including:

- **Republic Act No. 9175** (Chainsaw Act)
- **Republic Act No. 7586** (NIPAS Act)
- **Republic Act No. 8550** (Philippine Fisheries Code, as amended)
- **Republic Act No. 9003** (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act)
- Relevant DENR regulations and local ordinances of Tanay, Rizal

---

## Key Reminder

Reporting environmental crimes helps protect Tanay’s forests, rivers, and biodiversity. Early reporting allows authorities to respond quickly and prevent further environmental damage.
