# Municipality of Tanay: Free Health Services

The Municipality of Tanay provides accessible and free healthcare services through the **Municipal Health Office (MHO)** and barangay health centers, offering medical consultations, medicines, and vaccination programs for residents.

---

## 1. Free Medical Check-ups

| Service              | Where                                                   | Schedule                                       | Requirements                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General consultation | Municipal Health Office (MHO) & Barangay Health Centers | Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM (varies per barangay) | Patient registration / health record (for first-time patients) |

---

## 2. Free Basic Medicines

| Service                                          | Where                            | Schedule                 | Requirements                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Basic medicines                                  | Municipal Health Office Pharmacy | Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM | Doctor’s prescription or referral                |
| Priority medicines (indigent patients & seniors) | MHO / partner health programs    | As scheduled             | Valid ID, proof of residency, medical assessment |

---

## 3. Free Vaccines & Immunization

| Beneficiary       | Vaccine/Service                                       | Where                                | Schedule                                | Requirements                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Children          | Routine immunization (BCG, DPT, Polio, Measles, etc.) | Barangay Health Centers              | Immunization days per barangay schedule | Child health card / registration   |
| Pregnant women    | Tetanus toxoid & maternal vaccines                    | Barangay Health Centers              | During prenatal check-ups               | Prenatal record / maternal booklet |
| Animal bite cases | Anti-rabies vaccine                                   | Animal Bite Treatment Facility / MHO | Clinic hours                            | Patient registration               |

---

## How to Avail

1. **Check-ups** → Visit your nearest Barangay Health Center or the Municipal Health Office for consultation and registration.
2. **Medicines** → Bring a valid prescription from a licensed doctor or health worker.
3. **Child immunization** → Follow barangay schedules and bring the child’s health card.
4. **Pregnancy services** → Attend prenatal visits at the barangay health center for free vaccine and supplements.
5. **Anti-rabies** → Report to the Animal Bite Treatment Center; bring proof of residency.
6. **Senior citizen medicines** → Visit participating partner pharmacies; follow MAIFIPP guidelines.
