# Request Access to Water Supply Services in Tanay, Rizal

Residents of Tanay, Rizal who do not have access to private water connections may request assistance from their barangay water system, local water service providers, or the Municipal Government of Tanay.

Water services in Tanay may vary depending on the barangay and service coverage area.

---

## Options for Water Access

### 1. Barangay Water Supply Systems

Some barangays in Tanay operate community-based water systems or localized water distribution services.

Residents may coordinate with their Barangay Hall to:

- Apply for a water connection
- Report water interruptions or low pressure
- Request repair of water pipes or meters
- Ask about local water service schedules

---

### 2. Manila Water / Local Water Service Providers

Some areas in Rizal, including parts of Tanay, may be served by private or cooperative water service providers depending on coverage.

Residents may inquire about:

- New water service connections
- Meter installation
- Billing concerns
- Water interruptions and maintenance schedules

Availability depends on the location and existing infrastructure.

---

### 3. Alternative Water Assistance

For areas without stable piped water access, residents may seek assistance through:

- Barangay water delivery programs
- Municipal emergency water distribution
- Water trucking assistance during shortages or disasters
- DSWD or local relief assistance for indigent families during emergencies

---

## How to Apply for a Water Connection

1. Visit your Barangay Hall or the Municipal Hall
2. Ask about available water service providers in your area
3. Fill out the required application forms
4. Submit the necessary requirements:
   - Valid government-issued ID
   - Proof of residency
   - Proof of property ownership, occupancy, or lease
5. Pay applicable connection or installation fees if required
6. Wait for inspection and installation scheduling

---

## Report Water Issues

Residents may report the following concerns:

- No water supply
- Low water pressure
- Damaged pipes or leaks
- Water quality concerns
- Interrupted water service

Concerns may be reported through:

- Barangay Hall
- Municipal Government of Tanay
- Local water service provider
- Municipal Health Office for water sanitation concerns

---

**Municipal Government of Tanay**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

**Barangay Hall**
