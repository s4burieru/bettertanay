# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Tanay manages public market facilities and municipal cemetery services for residents, vendors, and small business owners in the municipality.

Residents may apply for public market stall rentals or coordinate burial and cemetery services through the appropriate municipal offices.

---

# Public Market Stalls

## Tanay Public Market

The Tanay Public Market provides affordable spaces for vendors selling:

- Fresh vegetables and fruits
- Meat and poultry
- Fish and seafood
- Cooked food and snacks
- Dry goods and general merchandise

Stall rentals are managed by the Municipal Government through the Market Administration Office and the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.

---

## To Apply for a Stall

1. Visit the Market Administration Office or Municipal Hall
2. Ask for a stall rental application form
3. Submit the required documents:
   - Valid government-issued ID
   - Barangay business clearance
   - Health certificate (for food vendors)
   - 2x2 ID photos
4. Pay the required rental and permit fees
5. Wait for stall assignment and approval

Availability of stalls depends on vacancy and market regulations.

---

## Requirements

- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
- Barangay clearance or business clearance
- Health certificate for food-related businesses
- Other permits required by the municipality

---

# LGU-Managed Cemetery

The Municipal Government of Tanay maintains public cemetery services for qualified residents of the municipality.

Burial lot assignments and cemetery-related transactions are commonly processed through the Municipal Treasurer’s Office or designated cemetery management personnel.

---

## To Apply for a Burial Lot

1. Visit the Municipal Treasurer’s Office or designated cemetery office
2. Present the death certificate of the deceased
3. Provide proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
4. Pay the applicable cemetery or burial fees
5. Receive the burial lot assignment or permit

Fees and requirements may vary depending on the type of lot or cemetery service requested.

---
