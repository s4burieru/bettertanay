# Use Public Facilities like Gyms, Barangay Halls, or Multipurpose Centers in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Tanay, Rizal maintains public facilities that residents can use for community activities, sports, training sessions, emergency response, and social events.

These facilities are managed through the Municipal Government and respective Barangay Halls.

---

## Available Public Facilities

### Municipal Gym / Sports Complex

Tanay has municipal sports facilities used for:

- Basketball games and tournaments
- Community sports events
- Training programs and fitness activities
- Emergency evacuation during disasters

Availability depends on scheduling and municipal events.

---

### Barangay Halls and Multipurpose Centers

Each barangay in Tanay has its own hall or multipurpose center that can be used for:

- Community meetings and assemblies
- Training and livelihood seminars
- Youth and sports activities
- Health services and outreach programs
- Elections and government services

Residents must coordinate directly with their Barangay Hall for reservations.

---

### Public Parks and Open Spaces

Tanay also offers public recreation areas such as:

- Plaza areas in poblacion and barangay centers
- River and eco-tourism areas (for designated public use)
- Open sports courts in barangays

Some eco-tourism sites may have guidelines or entrance rules depending on management.

---

## How to Reserve a Facility

1. Visit the Barangay Hall or Municipal Hall
2. Request a facility use or reservation form
3. Submit event details and purpose of use
4. Secure approval from the barangay captain or municipal office
5. Pay applicable fees if required
6. Follow scheduled usage guidelines

---

## Fees

- Many barangay facilities are free for official community and government activities
- Fees may apply for private events, tournaments, or commercial use
- Rates vary per barangay or facility type

Confirm details with the Barangay Hall or Municipal Government of Tanay.

---
