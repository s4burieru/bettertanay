# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage in Tanay, Rizal

Residents of Tanay, Rizal may report damaged public infrastructure such as roads, bridges, drainage systems, and street lights to the appropriate municipal or national government offices for inspection and repair.

The Municipal Government of Tanay coordinates infrastructure maintenance through the Municipal Engineering Office and other concerned agencies.

---

## What You Can Report

- Potholes and damaged road surfaces
- Broken or unsafe bridges and footbridges
- Clogged or damaged drainage canals
- Flood-prone or obstructed waterways
- Defective or missing street lights
- Damaged sidewalks and public pathways
- Landslides, eroded roads, or damaged embankments

---

## How to Report

### Option 1 — Visit the Municipal Office

**Municipal Engineering Office – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

You may personally report infrastructure concerns and request inspection or repair assistance.

---

### Option 2 — Report Through Your Barangay

Residents may also report issues to their Barangay Hall. Barangay officials can coordinate and endorse the concern to the Municipal Engineering Office or other agencies.

---

### Option 3 — Report National Road Concerns

For national roads and highways under DPWH jurisdiction, residents may contact:

**DPWH Rizal 1st District Engineering Office**  
Binangonan, Rizal, Philippines

National roads may include major highways and roads maintained by the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

---

## Information to Include

When filing a report, provide the following details if possible:

- Exact location of the problem
- Barangay and nearby landmarks
- Type of damage or issue
- Severity or safety risk involved
- Photos or videos for documentation
- Your name and contact number for follow-up

---

## Response Time

Response times may vary depending on:

- Severity of the damage
- Weather conditions
- Availability of municipal funds and equipment
- Whether the road is under LGU or DPWH jurisdiction

Emergency hazards and safety concerns are usually prioritized for immediate inspection.

---

## Follow-Up

Keep a copy or record of your report date and the office where you submitted the complaint for future follow-up and status inquiries.
