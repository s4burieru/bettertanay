# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipality of Tanay, Rizal implements socialized housing and relocation programs in coordination with the **National Housing Authority (NHA)** and other national government agencies to support informal settler families and residents living in danger zones.

These programs prioritize families affected by disasters, infrastructure projects, or hazardous living conditions.

---

## Who Can Apply

- Residents of Tanay living in **danger zones** (flood-prone, landslide-prone, or riverbank areas)
- Informal settler families (ISFs) without land ownership
- Low-income households classified as indigent or near-indigent
- Families displaced by government infrastructure projects or disasters
- Priority may be given to vulnerable households identified by barangays and LGU

---

## Requirements

- Valid government-issued IDs (applicant and spouse, if applicable)
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- Proof of income or Certificate of Indigency
- Marriage Certificate (if applicable)
- Birth Certificates of dependents
- Other documents required by the LGU or NHA
- Additional verification documents during evaluation (home visit or profiling)

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** or relevant LGU office
2. Request and accomplish the socialized housing application form
3. Submit complete documentary requirements
4. Undergo:
   - Household assessment
   - Income verification
   - Site validation or home visitation
5. Wait for evaluation and listing under available housing or relocation programs

---

## Available Programs

- **National Housing Authority (NHA) Resettlement Program** — for families in high-risk or disaster-prone areas
- **Community Mortgage Program (CMP)** — for organized informal settler communities
- **LGU Housing and Relocation Initiatives** — coordinated by the Municipal Government of Tanay
- **Disaster-affected Housing Assistance Programs** — for families displaced by calamities
