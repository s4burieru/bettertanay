# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits in Tanay, Rizal

Before constructing, renovating, or changing the use of land in **Tanay, Rizal**, you are required to secure permits and clearances from the Municipal Government, specifically through the **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)** and the **Office of the Building Official (OBO)**.

These processes ensure compliance with the local zoning ordinance and the National Building Code of the Philippines.

---

## Types of Permits

### 1. Locational Clearance / Zoning Certification

This confirms that your proposed land use or structure is allowed under Tanay’s zoning ordinance.

Required for:

- Residential construction
- Commercial buildings
- Land conversion or change of use
- Business establishment applications

---

### 2. Building Permit

Required before constructing, renovating, expanding, or demolishing any structure.

Common required forms (based on the National Building Code):

- Building Permit Application Form
- Architectural Plans (signed by licensed architect)
- Structural/Civil Design Plans
- Electrical Plans
- Mechanical Plans (if applicable)
- Sanitary/Plumbing Plans
- Fencing Permit (if applicable)

---

## General Requirements

- Duly accomplished application forms
- Lot plan or survey plan
- Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) or Tax Declaration
- Proof of ownership or authorization from owner
- Barangay Clearance
- Locational/Zoning Clearance
- Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (from BFP)

---

## Processing Steps

1. Submit complete documents to the **Office of the Building Official (OBO)**
2. Secure zoning clearance from the **MPDO**
3. Pay required fees at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office
4. Undergo technical evaluation and inspection
5. Receive approved permit before starting construction
6. Post the building permit visibly at the construction site

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

**Office of the Building Official (OBO)**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

**Municipal Treasurer’s Office**  
For payment and assessment of fees

---

## Reference Documents

- National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096)
- Local Zoning Ordinance of Tanay, Rizal
- Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) of Tanay

---

## Key Reminder

Always secure permits before starting any construction or land development. Unpermitted structures may be subject to fines, penalties, or demolition under local and national law.
