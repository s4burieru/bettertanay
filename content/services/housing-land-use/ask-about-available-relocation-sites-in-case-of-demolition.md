# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — Tanay, Rizal

If your household is facing demolition due to government infrastructure projects, road widening, danger zone clearing, or other public works, the Municipality of Tanay, Rizal follows the **Urban Development and Housing Act (Republic Act No. 7279)** in coordination with national housing agencies.

---

## Your Rights Under the Law

Under **RA 7279**, demolition or eviction should observe the following safeguards:

- Proper notice (generally at least 30 days before demolition)
- Coordination with local government units and relevant agencies
- Presence of authorized officials during execution
- Provision of **relocation or adequate assistance** for qualified beneficiaries
- Court order requirement for certain cases (especially private land disputes)

---

## Where to Inquire About Relocation Sites

You may coordinate with the following offices in Tanay:

### Municipal Government Offices

- Municipal Government of Tanay
- Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)
- Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)

Ask about:

- Available relocation or resettlement sites
- Ongoing housing or relocation programs
- Eligibility assessment for affected families

---

### National Housing Agencies

- National Housing Authority (NHA) — resettlement projects and housing units
- Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) — housing policy and coordination support

---

### Legal Assistance (If Needed)

- Public Attorney’s Office (PAO) — free legal assistance for qualified individuals
- Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) — oversight on LGU demolition procedures

---

## Steps to Take if Facing Demolition

1. Coordinate first with your **Barangay Hall**
2. Visit the MSWDO Tanay to register as an affected family
3. Request written notice and relocation details
4. Ask for assessment of eligibility for relocation programs
5. Seek legal advice from PAO if rights are unclear or disputed

---

## Requirements (Commonly Requested)

- Valid government-issued ID
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- Proof of occupancy (utility bills, certification, etc.)
- Family composition documents (birth/marriage certificates)
- Certificate of Indigency (if applicable)

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Government of Tanay**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

**MSWDO / MPDO Offices**  
Located within the Municipal Hall — coordinate through the front desk or information office

**Barangay Hall**  
First point of contact for endorsement and verification

---

## Key Reminder

Do not ignore demolition notices. Always coordinate with your barangay and the MSWDO to ensure your rights under **RA 7279** are properly observed and that relocation assistance is properly provided.
