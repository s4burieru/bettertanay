# Municipality of Tanay: Family and Community Welfare Support

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of the Municipality of Tanay provides programs and services for families, children, women, youth, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, and individuals in crisis situations.

These services are delivered through the Municipal Government in coordination with barangays, schools, health offices, law enforcement agencies, and national government programs.

---

# How to Access MSWDO Services

## Step 1 — Visit Your Barangay Hall

Your barangay is usually the first point of contact.

The Barangay Social Welfare Office may assist with:

- Barangay endorsements
- Certificate of Indigency
- Certificate of Residency
- Initial intake and referrals

---

## Step 2 — Prepare Your Documents

Bring applicable documents such as:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Barangay Certificate or referral
- Medical records or hospital bills
- Police or incident reports (if applicable)
- Birth certificates or supporting documents

Requirements may vary depending on the service requested.

---

## Step 3 — Proceed to the MSWDO Office

Visit the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** at the Tanay Municipal Hall for assessment and processing.

A social worker may conduct an interview and evaluation before approval of assistance or referral.

---

## Step 4 — Follow Instructions and Return if Needed

Some services may be processed immediately, while others may require follow-up visits, verification, or additional documents.

---

# Important Reminders

- Barangay referrals and endorsements are highly recommended.
- Complete documents help avoid delays in processing.
- Assistance is subject to evaluation, qualification, and availability of municipal or national government funds.
- Emergency and urgent cases are prioritized when possible.

---

# Important Offices and Agencies

## Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Handles social welfare services, crisis intervention, and assistance programs.

## Barangay Social Welfare Office (BSWO)

Provides barangay-level referrals, certifications, and initial screening.

## Philippine National Police (PNP) – Women and Children Protection Desk

Handles VAWC complaints and protection cases.

## Public Attorney’s Office (PAO)

Provides free legal assistance to qualified individuals.

## Rural Health Unit (RHU) / Municipal Health Office

Provides medical certification and health-related services.

---

# Notes

- Procedures and requirements may change depending on municipal policies and national government guidelines.
- Residents are encouraged to coordinate first with their barangay for updated information and proper referrals.
