# Municipality of Tanay: Assistance for Senior Citizens, Solo Parents, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs)

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of the Municipality of Tanay provides welfare and social assistance programs for qualified residents, including senior citizens, solo parents, and persons with disabilities (PWDs).

These services are implemented in coordination with barangays, national government agencies, and local offices such as the **Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)** and the **Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)**.

---

# Senior Citizen Welfare Programs

## Who May Qualify

- Residents of Tanay aged **60 years old and above**
- Must be a bona fide resident of the Municipality of Tanay

---

## Available Benefits and Services

| Benefit / Service     | Description                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Senior Citizen ID     | Required to access discounts and privileges under the Expanded Senior Citizens Act |
| OSCA Registration     | Registration under the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs                          |
| Social Pension (DSWD) | Financial assistance for indigent senior citizens subject to DSWD qualification    |
| Medical Assistance    | Assistance for medicines, hospitalization, and medical needs                       |
| Burial Assistance     | Financial support for indigent senior citizens                                     |
| Community Programs    | Social, health, and recreational activities for seniors                            |

---

## Requirements

Typical requirements may include:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Birth Certificate or proof of age
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- Recent ID photos

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** for residency verification and endorsement.
2. Proceed to the **OSCA Office** or MSWDO for registration.
3. Submit the required documents.
4. Wait for evaluation and processing of your ID or assistance request.

---

# Solo Parent Welfare Programs

## Who May Qualify

Applicants who can prove solo parent status due to:

- Death of spouse
- Legal separation or annulment
- Abandonment
- Overseas absence of spouse
- Unmarried parenthood
- Other situations recognized under the Solo Parents Welfare Act

---

## Available Benefits and Services

| Benefit / Service    | Description                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Solo Parent ID       | Required to access benefits and priority services      |
| Financial Assistance | Emergency or supplemental assistance                   |
| Livelihood Programs  | Access to livelihood and skills training opportunities |
| Counseling Services  | Family and psychosocial support                        |
| Referral Assistance  | Legal and social service referrals                     |

---

## Requirements

Typical requirements may include:

- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- PSA Birth Certificate of child/children
- Valid ID
- Proof of solo parent status
- Recent ID photos

Additional documents may be required depending on the applicant’s situation.

---

## How to Apply

1. Secure barangay certification and endorsement.
2. Submit complete documents to the **MSWDO Tanay**.
3. Undergo assessment and interview by a social worker.
4. Wait for approval and issuance of Solo Parent ID.

---

# Persons with Disabilities (PWD) Services

## Who May Qualify

Residents with certified disabilities, including:

- Physical disabilities
- Visual impairments
- Hearing impairments
- Intellectual disabilities
- Psychosocial disabilities
- Other medically recognized disabilities

---

## Available Benefits and Services

| Benefit / Service           | Description                                                       |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PWD ID                      | Grants access to discounts and privileges under Philippine law    |
| Financial Assistance        | Emergency and supplemental aid                                    |
| Assistive Devices           | Wheelchairs and other assistive equipment subject to availability |
| Livelihood Support          | Access to livelihood and employment programs                      |
| Social Inclusion Activities | Community participation and awareness programs                    |

---

## Requirements

Typical requirements may include:

- Medical Certificate or Disability Certificate from a licensed physician
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- Valid ID of applicant or guardian
- Recent ID photos

---

## How to Apply

1. Obtain a medical certificate confirming the disability.
2. Visit your Barangay Hall for residency certification and endorsement.
3. Submit documents to the **MSWDO** or designated PWD office.
4. Wait for assessment and approval.

---

# General Process for MSWDO Assistance

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** first for endorsements or certifications.
2. Prepare all required documents before visiting the Municipal Hall.
3. Proceed to the **MSWDO Office** at the Municipal Hall.
4. Undergo assessment or interview by a social worker.
5. Wait for evaluation and approval of assistance.
6. Return on the scheduled release or follow-up date if required.

---

# Important Offices

## Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Handles social welfare programs and assistance services.

## Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)

Handles senior citizen registration and related programs.

## Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)

Handles PWD registration and assistance.

## Barangay Social Welfare Office (BSWO)

First point of contact for certifications, referrals, and endorsements.

---

# Notes

- Assistance programs are subject to availability of municipal or national government funds.
- Requirements and procedures may change depending on municipal policies and national guidelines.
- Applicants are encouraged to coordinate first with their barangay for updated requirements and referrals.
