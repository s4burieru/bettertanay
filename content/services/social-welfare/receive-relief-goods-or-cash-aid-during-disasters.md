# Municipality of Tanay: Relief Goods and Cash Aid During Disasters

During emergencies such as typhoons, flooding, fires, landslides, and other disasters, the **Municipality of Tanay** provides relief and emergency assistance through the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)**, the **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)**, and your **Barangay Local Government Unit**.

---

# How Relief Distribution Works

Relief operations in Tanay are coordinated from the national level down to the barangay level.

| Level                            | Role                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DSWD (National Government)       | Provides relief supplies, emergency funding, and augmentation support   |
| MDRRMO (Municipal Level)         | Leads disaster response, evacuation, and coordination                   |
| MSWDO (Municipal Social Welfare) | Handles social welfare assistance, AICS assessment, and case management |
| Barangay LGU / BSWO              | First responder for relief distribution and beneficiary listing         |

Barangays are typically the **main distribution points** for relief goods.

---

# Types of Disaster Assistance

| Assistance                | Details                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relief Goods              | Food packs such as rice, canned goods, and basic necessities               |
| Emergency Cash Assistance | Financial aid for affected or displaced families (subject to assessment)   |
| Evacuation Support        | Temporary shelter, evacuation centers, and basic needs support             |
| AICS Assistance           | Medical, burial, food, and transportation assistance for crisis situations |
| Referral Assistance       | Coordination with DSWD and partner agencies for additional aid             |

---

# Who Can Receive Relief Assistance

Priority beneficiaries during disasters include:

- Families affected by typhoons or flooding
- Fire victims and displaced households
- Residents affected by landslides or severe weather events
- Families with destroyed or damaged homes
- Individuals with sudden loss of income due to disasters

---

# How to Receive Relief Goods (Barangay Level)

1. **Wait for official barangay announcements**
   - Updates are released via barangay officials, text blasts, or official Facebook pages.

2. **Report your situation to your Barangay Hall**
   - Inform the Barangay Captain or Barangay Social Welfare Officer (BSWO).

3. **Register as an affected family**
   - Provide your name and details for the barangay master list.

4. **Present a valid ID (if required)**

5. **Claim relief goods**
   - Distribution is usually done at the barangay hall, covered court, or designated evacuation/distribution centers.

---

# How to Apply for Emergency Cash Assistance (MSWDO / AICS)

1. **Visit your Barangay Hall immediately after the incident**
   - Request:
     - Certificate of Indigency
     - Incident report or certification (fire, flood, or disaster report)

2. **Prepare required documents for MSWDO:**
   - Valid government-issued ID
   - Barangay indigency or residency certificate
   - Incident report (from barangay or responding agency like BFP/MDRRMO if applicable)
   - Proof of loss (photos, damaged property records, medical bills if applicable)

3. **Proceed to the MSWDO Office at Tanay Municipal Hall**
   - Submit documents for assessment

4. **Social worker evaluation**
   - A social worker will assess urgency and eligibility under AICS or other assistance programs

5. **Release of assistance**
   - May be cash aid, guarantee letter, or referral depending on evaluation and available funds

---

# Medical Emergencies During Disasters

For disaster-related injuries or hospitalization:

- Visit the nearest government or partner hospital
- You may be assisted through **Malasakit Centers** (where available)
  - These coordinate assistance from DSWD, DOH, PhilHealth, and PCSO in one location

---

# Important Reminders

- **Barangay endorsement is essential** — always coordinate with your barangay first
- **Complete documents speed up processing** — missing reports may delay assistance
- **Assistance is not always cash** — some cases receive goods, guarantee letters, or referrals instead
- **Processing depends on assessment and available funds**

---

# Key Offices and Contacts

## Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)

Handles emergency response, evacuation, and disaster coordination.

## Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Handles AICS applications, cash assistance, and social welfare support.

## Barangay Local Government Unit / BSWO

First point of contact for relief distribution and initial reporting.

## Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)

Issues fire incident reports required for fire-related assistance cases.

---

# Notes

- Disaster response procedures may vary depending on the severity of the situation.
- Relief distribution is prioritized based on urgency and vulnerability.
- Residents are encouraged to monitor official barangay and municipal announcements during emergencies.
