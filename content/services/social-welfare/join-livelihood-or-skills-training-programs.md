# Municipality of Tanay: Livelihood and Skills Training Programs (Social Welfare)

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of the Municipality of Tanay provides livelihood assistance and skills training programs for vulnerable and low-income residents.

These programs are implemented in coordination with barangays and partner agencies such as **TESDA**, **DOLE**, **DTI**, **PESO**, and the **Negosyo Center Tanay**.

> For broader entrepreneurship, employment, and business assistance programs, refer to the municipality’s livelihood, employment, and MSME support services.

---

# Priority Beneficiaries

The following groups are commonly prioritized for social welfare livelihood programs:

| Priority Group                            | Notes                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Indigent Families                         | Primary beneficiaries of MSWDO assistance                        |
| 4Ps Beneficiaries                         | Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program members may receive priority |
| Solo Parents                              | Priority assistance under the Solo Parents Welfare Act           |
| Persons with Disabilities (PWDs)          | Included in livelihood and social inclusion programs             |
| Senior Citizens                           | Select livelihood activities for active senior citizens          |
| Unemployed Residents                      | May be referred through PESO and barangay programs               |
| Women’s Groups and Community Associations | May receive group livelihood assistance                          |

---

# Types of Livelihood Assistance

## Starter Kits and Capital Assistance

Qualified beneficiaries may receive:

- Livelihood starter kits
- Basic tools and equipment
- Small livelihood materials
- Food vending or food processing kits
- Sewing or tailoring materials

Distribution depends on available municipal or partner agency programs.

---

# Skills Training Programs

Training programs may include:

| Training Area             | Examples                                       |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Food Processing           | Baking, cooking, food preparation              |
| Livelihood Skills         | Tailoring, handicrafts, small-scale retail     |
| Agriculture and Gardening | Urban gardening and sustainable farming        |
| Technical Skills          | TESDA-supported livelihood training            |
| Entrepreneurship          | Basic business and financial literacy seminars |

Programs may be conducted at:

- Barangay halls
- Covered courts
- Community centers
- Municipal training venues

---

# Community-Based Livelihood Projects

Some livelihood programs are organized through barangays and community organizations.

Examples may include:

- Community gardening
- Cooperative livelihood projects
- Women’s livelihood associations
- Barangay-based production activities

These are often facilitated by barangay officials and Barangay Social Welfare Offices (BSWOs).

---

# Employment and Job Readiness Support

The municipality may coordinate with the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** for:

- Job matching and referrals
- Resume and interview preparation
- Employment coaching
- Skills assessment and career guidance
- Referral to local employers and job fairs

---

# How to Apply

## Step 1 — Visit Your Barangay Hall

Ask the Barangay Social Welfare Office (BSWO) or barangay officials about:

- Ongoing livelihood programs
- Available training schedules
- Beneficiary screening and listing

---

## Step 2 — Prepare the Required Documents

Typical requirements may include:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Barangay Certificate of Indigency or Residency
- Proof of eligibility (if applicable):
  - 4Ps ID
  - Solo Parent ID
  - PWD ID
  - Senior Citizen ID

Requirements may vary depending on the program.

---

## Step 3 — Register and Attend Orientation or Training

Some programs require:

- Attendance in seminars or orientation
- Completion of skills training
- Participation in community activities

Training participation is often required before release of starter kits or livelihood assistance.

---

## Step 4 — Release of Assistance or Referral

Qualified participants may receive:

- Livelihood starter kits
- Certificates of training completion
- Referral to partner agencies
- Employment or business support services

---

# Important Reminders

- Livelihood programs are not always available year-round.
- Program schedules and funding depend on municipal and partner agency allocations.
- Slots may be limited and prioritized for qualified beneficiaries.
- Residents are encouraged to monitor barangay announcements and municipal advisories.

---

# Partner Offices and Agencies

## Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Main office for social welfare livelihood assistance.

## Barangay Social Welfare Office (BSWO)

Handles barangay-level screening, referrals, and community programs.

## Public Employment Service Office (PESO)

Provides employment assistance and job readiness programs.

## Negosyo Center Tanay

Provides MSME, entrepreneurship, and business support services.

## TESDA

Provides technical and vocational skills training programs.

## Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE)

Supports livelihood and emergency employment assistance programs.

## Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)

Provides entrepreneurship and small business support.

---

# Notes

- Availability of programs may change depending on municipal priorities and funding.
- Applicants are encouraged to coordinate with their barangay first for updated information and referrals.
