# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) of Tanay, Rizal provides agricultural assistance programs and farming support to local farmers, fisherfolk, and residents engaged in backyard gardening and food production.

These programs are commonly supported through partnerships with the Department of Agriculture (DA) CALABARZON and other government agricultural initiatives.

---

## What is Available

- **Vegetable Seeds** — pechay, sitaw, mustasa, kangkong, ampalaya, and other seasonal crops
- **Seedlings** — fruit-bearing trees and planting materials
- **Fertilizers** — organic and inorganic fertilizers depending on program availability
- **Fingerlings** — tilapia and other freshwater fish fingerlings for aquaculture projects
- **Agricultural Assistance** — farm tools, technical support, and livelihood interventions for qualified farmer groups

The Department of Agriculture has previously distributed seeds and agricultural support in Tanay barangays under food security and livelihood programs.

---

## Who Can Apply

- Registered farmers and fisherfolk in Tanay
- Residents with backyard gardens or small farm lots
- Farmer associations and cooperatives
- Qualified beneficiaries under DA or LGU agricultural programs

---

## Requirements

Requirements may vary depending on the program, but commonly include:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
- Barangay certification
- Farm or garden information
- Registration under the **RSBSA (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)** for some assistance programs

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Tanay Municipal Hall
2. Ask about available agricultural assistance programs
3. Fill out the required forms
4. Submit the necessary documents
5. Wait for the schedule of release or distribution

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

You may also coordinate with your Barangay Hall for announcements regarding seed distribution, planting programs, and agricultural assistance.

---

## Schedule

Distribution schedules usually happen during:

- Planting seasons
- DA assistance rollouts
- Municipal agricultural programs
- Special livelihood and food security projects

Availability depends on LGU and Department of Agriculture program allocations.
