# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) of Tanay, Rizal provides veterinary and animal health services to support local farmers, backyard raisers, and livestock owners in the municipality.

These services help prevent animal diseases, improve livestock health, and support agricultural livelihood programs in Tanay.

---

## Services Available

- **Vaccination** — hog cholera, rabies, Newcastle disease, and other livestock or poultry vaccinations
- **Deworming** — for cattle, goats, pigs, poultry, and other farm animals
- **Castration** — for male livestock animals
- **Animal Treatment** — basic diagnosis and treatment for sick animals
- **Animal Health Consultation** — livestock management and disease prevention advice
- **Rabies Vaccination Campaigns** — for dogs and cats during scheduled municipal programs

---

## Who Can Avail

- Residents of Tanay, Rizal with livestock or poultry
- Registered farmers and backyard animal raisers
- Pet owners during municipal anti-rabies campaigns
- Farmer associations and cooperatives

---

## Requirements

- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
- Animal or livestock information (if applicable)

---

## How to Avail

1. Visit the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Tanay Municipal Hall
2. Ask for available veterinary or livestock services
3. Schedule a farm visit for large animals if necessary
4. Bring small animals during scheduled veterinary service days or vaccination drives

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

Residents may also coordinate with their Barangay Hall for scheduled vaccination drives and veterinary outreach programs.

---

## Schedule

Veterinary services and vaccination campaigns are conducted on scheduled dates depending on municipal programs and barangay requests.

Announcements are usually posted through:

- Barangay Halls
- Municipal Agriculture Office
- Official LGU social media pages
