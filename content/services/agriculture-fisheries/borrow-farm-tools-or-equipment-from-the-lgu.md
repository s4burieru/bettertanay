# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment from the LGU in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) of Tanay, Rizal provides assistance and access to agricultural tools and equipment for local farmers through municipal agriculture support programs.

These programs help improve farm productivity and support small-scale and subsistence farmers in the municipality.

---

## Equipment Available for Borrowing

Depending on availability and municipal programs, farmers may access:

- Hand tractors / power tillers
- Knapsack and motorized sprayers
- Harvesting equipment
- Post-harvest tools and machinery
- Garden and farming tools
- Other agricultural equipment distributed through DA or LGU programs

Equipment availability may vary depending on the season, barangay requests, and current municipal resources.

---

## Who Can Borrow

- Registered farmers in Tanay, Rizal
- Members of accredited farmers' associations or cooperatives
- Small-scale and subsistence farmers
- Qualified agricultural beneficiaries endorsed by barangays or farmer groups

---

## Requirements

Requirements may vary depending on the equipment and program, but commonly include:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
- Barangay certification as a farmer or resident
- Registration with the Municipal Agriculture Office
- Signed borrower’s agreement or request form

---

## How to Borrow

1. Visit the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Tanay Municipal Hall
2. Ask about available farm tools or equipment for borrowing
3. Fill out the required request or borrower’s form
4. Coordinate the release, pickup, and return schedule
5. Follow the equipment usage and return guidelines provided by the office

---

## Terms of Use

- Equipment must be returned on the agreed schedule
- Borrowers are responsible for proper handling and safe use
- Damage caused by misuse may require repair or replacement
- Late returns may affect future borrowing privileges

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

Residents may also coordinate with their Barangay Hall or local farmer associations regarding equipment availability and scheduling.
