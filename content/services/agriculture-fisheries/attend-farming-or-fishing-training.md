# Attend Farming or Fishing Training in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) of Tanay, Rizal, in coordination with the Department of Agriculture (DA), Agricultural Training Institute (ATI), and other government agencies, conducts free agricultural and livelihood training programs for farmers, fisherfolk, and residents.

These programs aim to improve farming productivity, sustainable agriculture practices, aquaculture development, and agricultural livelihood opportunities in the municipality.

---

## Training Topics Available

Training programs may include:

- **Organic Farming** — composting, natural pesticides, and soil management
- **Vegetable Production** — crop production techniques and backyard gardening
- **Aquaculture** — tilapia culture, fishpond management, and fingerling production
- **Livestock and Poultry Raising** — animal health and proper management
- **Post-Harvest Handling** — storage, packaging, and product marketing
- **Farm Mechanization** — operation and maintenance of farm equipment
- **Agribusiness and Livelihood** — bookkeeping, cooperative management, and entrepreneurship
- **Climate-Resilient Farming** — sustainable and disaster-resilient agricultural practices

Training availability depends on current municipal and DA programs.

---

## Who Can Join

- Registered farmers and fisherfolk in Tanay, Rizal
- Members of farmers’ and fisherfolk organizations
- Residents involved in farming, gardening, aquaculture, or livestock raising
- Youth and community members interested in agriculture-based livelihood programs

---

## Requirements

Requirements may vary depending on the training program, but commonly include:

- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of residency in Tanay, Rizal
- Registration with the Municipal Agriculture Office (recommended)
- Attendance confirmation for scheduled training sessions

---

## How to Register

1. Visit the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Tanay Municipal Hall
2. Ask about upcoming agricultural or fisheries training schedules
3. Submit your name and contact information for registration
4. Attend the orientation or scheduled training session

Some trainings may also be coordinated through barangays, farmer associations, cooperatives, or DA field offices.

---

## Where to Go

**Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

Training venues may vary and can include:

- Municipal Hall
- Barangay Halls
- Demonstration farms
- Agricultural training sites
