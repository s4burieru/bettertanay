# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipality of Tanay, Rizal, through the **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)**, has established emergency response systems and evacuation planning procedures due to risks from flooding, landslides, earthquakes, and severe typhoons.

Residents are strongly encouraged to familiarize themselves with evacuation routes and emergency protocols before disasters occur.

## Evacuation Routes

Evacuation routes in Tanay depend on your barangay location and the type of hazard (flood, landslide, or earthquake).

### Common evacuation directions include:

- Move to **higher ground or safer upland areas** during flooding
- Avoid **river systems, creeks, and low-lying zones**
- Proceed to **designated barangay or municipal evacuation centers**

### Common evacuation sites include:

- Barangay covered courts
- Public schools
- Municipal gym or designated open areas
- Safer upland barangays (for flood-prone communities)

Evacuation maps are typically available at Barangay Halls and during MDRRMO community briefings.

---

## Hazard Awareness Guidelines

### Flooding

- Avoid rivers, creeks, and low-lying areas
- Move to higher ground immediately when water levels rise

### Landslides

- Evacuate immediately during prolonged heavy rainfall
- Avoid steep slopes and areas with visible ground cracks

### Earthquakes

- Drop, Cover, and Hold
- Move to open areas once shaking stops

---

## What to Do During a Disaster

1. Follow instructions from MDRRMO and barangay officials
2. Evacuate immediately when advised
3. Bring your prepared emergency “Go Bag”
4. Stay in designated evacuation centers
5. Monitor official LGU announcements and advisories
