# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning in Tanay, Rizal

The Municipality of Tanay, Rizal, through the **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)**, conducts regular disaster preparedness activities to ensure residents are ready for emergencies such as typhoons, floods, landslides, and earthquakes.

Due to Tanay’s mountainous terrain and river systems, preparedness drills and evacuation planning are essential for community safety.

---

## Types of Drills Conducted

- **Earthquake Drill** — “Duck, Cover, and Hold” procedures and evacuation to safe zones
- **Flood Drill** — evacuation from low-lying and river-adjacent areas
- **Landslide Drill** — evacuation procedures in upland and slope-prone barangays
- **Fire Drill** — emergency response and evacuation procedures for residential and public areas
- **Multi-Hazard Simulation Drills** — combined disaster response exercises with LGU, schools, and barangays

---

## How to Participate

1. Watch announcements from:
   - Barangay Hall
   - MDRRMO Tanay
   - Official Tanay LGU pages

2. Schools, workplaces, and community organizations may coordinate directly with MDRRMO for scheduled drills
3. Participate actively during scheduled community or barangay-wide drills
4. Follow instructions from trained responders and barangay officials

---

## Evacuation Planning

Each barangay in Tanay has designated **evacuation centers** and routes for different types of disasters.

Common evacuation sites may include:

- Barangay covered courts
- Public school buildings
- Municipal gym or designated open grounds
- Designated safer zones identified by MDRRMO

Evacuation maps are typically available at Barangay Halls and during community orientations.

## Tips for Residents

- Know your nearest evacuation center in advance
- Memorize your barangay evacuation route
- Prepare a “Go Bag” with essentials:
  - Water and food supply
  - Flashlight and batteries
  - First aid kit and medicines
  - Important documents in waterproof bag
- Stay updated through official barangay and LGU announcements

---

## Key Reminder

Disaster drills save lives. Participation ensures you and your family know exactly what to do during real emergencies in Tanay, Rizal.
