# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems — Tanay, Rizal

The Municipality of Tanay, Rizal, through the **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)**, provides disaster preparedness programs, early warning systems, and emergency response services to protect residents from natural and human-induced hazards.

Tanay is prone to hazards such as flooding, landslides, earthquakes, and strong typhoons due to its terrain and river systems.

---

## Early Warning Systems Available

### 1. Weather Warnings

- The **PAGASA** issues typhoon signals, rainfall advisories, and flood warnings
- Residents are advised to monitor official updates via radio, TV, and government announcements
- Barangays may issue local flood or landslide alerts during heavy rainfall

---

### 2. River, Flood, and Landslide Monitoring

- MDRRMO monitors rivers, creeks, and landslide-prone areas
- Warning systems include:
  - Barangay sirens (where available)
  - Megaphone or public address systems
  - Barangay text/SMS or social media alerts

---

### 3. Earthquake Alerts

- The **PHIVOLCS** provides earthquake information and seismic updates
- Local governments assist in disseminating safety advisories
- Barangay evacuation protocols are activated after significant tremors

---

## Preparedness Materials

You may request disaster preparedness information from:

- MDRRMO Tanay
- Your Barangay Hall

Common materials include:

- Family disaster preparedness guides
- Evacuation route maps
- Flood and landslide hazard maps
- Emergency contact lists
- “Go Bag” checklists

---

## How to Register for Alerts

1. Visit your Barangay Hall
2. Provide your mobile number for emergency SMS alerts (if available in your barangay system)
3. Follow official LGU and barangay social media pages for updates
4. Stay tuned to MDRRMO announcements during typhoons or disasters

---

## MDRRMO Office

**Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) – Tanay, Rizal**  
Municipal Hall, Tanay, Rizal, Philippines

The MDRRMO operates 24/7 for emergency response, rescue operations, and disaster coordination.

---

## Build Your Go-Bag

Prepare an emergency kit with the following essentials:

- Drinking water (at least 3 days supply)
- Ready-to-eat or canned food
- Flashlight and extra batteries
- First aid kit and personal medicines
- Important documents in waterproof container
- Cash in small denominations
- Face masks (for ashfall or dust exposure)
- Extra clothes and blanket
- Power bank for mobile devices

---

## Key Reminder

Disaster preparedness saves lives. Always stay alert to official warnings and follow evacuation orders from the MDRRMO and your Barangay officials immediately when needed.
